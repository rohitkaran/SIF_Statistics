# SIFintel — intro-to-SIF video scripts (for Higgsfield)

Three ready-to-produce short-form videos (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / LinkedIn). Each has a
hook, full voice-over (VO), on-screen captions, and **per-scene Higgsfield prompts**. Produce each
scene as a 3–6s clip in Higgsfield, stitch in order, add the VO + burned-in captions + the SIFintel
end card.

**Look:** *"Premium fintech, dark UI. Deep midnight background (#0d1014) with an indigo→violet→teal

**Music:** minimal, modern, building; drop the beat on the hook line.

> A Specialized Investment Fund can also *short*: bet against the stocks it thinks will fall.
> So when the Nifty drops, its shorts make money — cushioning the fall, sometimes even turning it into
> a gain.
> We measure this as "down capture". Some SIFs post *negative* down capture — they rose while the

## Video 3 — "Long-short in 30 seconds" (concept explainer)

**Hook (0–3s):** "Buy the winners, bet against the losers — at the same time. That's a long-short fund."

**VO (full):**
> Here's the idea behind most SIFs, in thirty seconds. It's called long-short.
> "Long" means you *own* a stock — you win if it rises. "Short" means you *bet against* one — you win
> if it falls.
> A long-short fund does both at once: it owns the companies it believes in, and shorts the ones it
> doesn't.
> The magic is that the returns come from the *gap* between the two — the manager's skill — not from
> the market going up.
> So it can make money in flat or falling markets, and take smaller hits when things get rough.
> That's the edge. See how each fund actually does it on SIFintel.

**On-screen captions:** "Long-short, explained" → "LONG = you own it ↑" → "SHORT = you bet against ↓" →
"Do BOTH at once" → "Profit = the GAP between them" → "Works even in flat markets" → "How? → SIFintel"

**Higgsfield scenes:**
1. *(hook)* Two glowing arrows on dark gradient — green up, red down — crossing in an X; premium. Text:
   "Long-short".
2. A rising stock with a "+" and a person holding it; clean 3D icon; push-in. Text: "LONG ↑ — you own it".
3. A falling stock with a "–" and a downward bet lighting up teal. Text: "SHORT ↓ — bet against it".
4. The two arrows merge into one balanced scale/beam glowing at the center. Text: "Do both".
5. A widening luminous "gap" between the long line and short line, filling with light. Text: "Profit =
   the gap".
6. A flat/sideways market line while the fund line still climbs; subtle. Text: "Even in flat markets".
7. *(end card)* SIFintel wordmark + URL.

**Post caption:** Long-short, in 30 seconds: own the winners, short the losers, profit from the gap.
It's how most SIFs work — compare them all at sifintel.com. *Educational, not advice.*
