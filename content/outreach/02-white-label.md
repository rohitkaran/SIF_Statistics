## The 20-minute call, structured

1. **Two minutes:** share the live site, let them click. Do not present — let them find something.
2. **Five minutes:** ask what their current process is and who does it by hand. Write down the
   number of person-days a month; that is the ROI number you will quote back.
3. **Five minutes:** show the pipeline failing safely — the monitor, the alert, the fallback to last
   good data. This is what separates a real build from a demo, and it is what buyers have been burned
   on before.
4. **Five minutes:** scope one concrete first deliverable. Never quote the whole platform on the
   first call; quote the smallest thing that is useful on its own.
5. **Three minutes:** agree the next step with a date on it.

## Pricing discipline

- Quote fixed scope, fixed price. Hourly invites scope arguments you will lose.
- The ₹85,000 analytics review exists to be said yes to quickly. It is the door, not the revenue —
  it turns a cold prospect into a client whose data you now understand.
- Do not discount the first deal to win it. Reduce scope instead; a cheap precedent prices every
  later engagement.
