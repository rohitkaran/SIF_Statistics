# SIFintel — content compliance policy

> **This is internal guidance, not legal advice.** SIFintel is not (yet) a SEBI-registered Investment
> Adviser (RIA), Research Analyst (RA), or distributor (ARN). Before publishing at scale, have a
> **SEBI-experienced securities lawyer / compliance consultant** review this policy, the website, and a
> sample of the content. When in doubt, leave it out.

## Our position
SIFintel is a **data + education** platform. We publish *general educational information* and provide a
*neutral tool* that displays *publicly disclosed, factual* data (NAVs, holdings, standard risk/return
math) that users look up themselves. We do **not** give investment advice, make recommendations, or
promote any specific scheme.

## ✅ Safe (do this)
- **Generic education:** what a SIF is, why SEBI created the category, the 5 strategy families, and
  concepts (long-short, hedging, arbitrage, drawdown, up/down capture, Sharpe/Sortino) — explained
  **without naming a specific fund as good/bad**.
- **Neutral, factual data tool:** the dashboard shows public data with an **"as-of" date** and a
  **source** for every number, and lets the *user* sort/compare. No editorial "best/winner/avoid".
- **Standard disclaimer** on every public piece (see below).
- **Attribution:** every figure traces to AMFI / the AMC's own disclosure.

## ⚠️ Avoid until appropriately licensed (RA/RIA/ARN)
- Recommendations or calls to action for a **specific scheme**: "best SIF", "top pick", "buy X",
  "invest in X now".
- **Marketing that ranks or highlights specific funds' performance** ("Fund X is up 8%!",
  "Fund X beat the Nifty") in videos, reels, posts, ads, or thumbnails — this can be read as
  inducement/promotion.
- Implying **assured/expected returns**, guarantees, or downplaying risk.
- **Personalised advice** ("you should invest…"), testimonials, or comparative advertising that
  steers viewers into a particular fund.
- Cherry-picked timeframes or figures that mislead.

**Key distinction:** a *user querying a neutral information tool* (factual public data, no editorial
slant) is different from *us publishing marketing that promotes a fund*. Keep **public marketing
content fund-agnostic and educational**; keep fund-specific data **inside the tool**, factual and
user-initiated.

## Standard disclaimer (use verbatim)
On every video end-card, blog footer, and social caption:
> *Educational content, not investment advice. SIFintel is not a SEBI-registered adviser, research
> analyst, or distributor. Specialized Investment Funds carry high risk and a ₹10 lakh minimum. Read
> the scheme documents and consult a SEBI-registered adviser before investing.*

## Process
1. Draft against this policy. 2. Human review (does it recommend/rank/promote a specific fund? if yes,
rewrite). 3. Add the disclaimer. 4. Publish. 5. Keep a record of what was published.

## Roadmap to doing more
If/when you want to publish fund-specific analysis, ratings, or advice, get the matching SEBI
registration first (**RA** for research/ratings, **RIA** for advice, **ARN** for distribution) and
follow the SEBI Advertisement Code. Until then: **education + neutral tool only.**
