Two legs, one quarter — the ten SIFs that were up in both

The market gave managers a clean natural experiment this quarter: a 5.85% rally into early August, then a 3.47% fall. Of the 21 SIF schemes with NAVs covering both legs, ten finished each of them in the black.

The NIFTY fell 3.5% in a month. Every long-short SIF fell less.

Between 3 August and 2 September 2026 the NIFTY 50 lost 3.47%. Across the 30 Indian SIF schemes with a full month of published NAVs, not one lost that much — and 18 of them finished the month higher.
