SIF data & API

The complete NAV and portfolio history of India's Specialized Investment Funds, maintained daily, available as a JSON API or a flat file. If you are building a product, a report or a research process that has to know what SIFs are actually doing, this is the dataset behind it.

What's in it

  • NAV history for every SIF scheme AMFI publishes, from launch to yesterday, with scheme code, ISIN, fund house and SEBI category.
  • Disclosed portfolios — the holdings each fund house publishes, normalised into one schema instead of 4 different spreadsheet and PDF layouts.
  • Benchmark series (NIFTY 50) aligned to the same dates, so capture ratios and relative returns are computable without a second vendor.

Try it right now — no key needed

The free tier is live and needs no signup. It returns 30 days for a single scheme, which is enough to see the shape of the data:

curl "https://www.sifintel.com/api/v1/meta"
curl "https://www.sifintel.com/api/v1/schemes?q=long-short"
curl "https://www.sifintel.com/api/v1/nav?code=SIF-120"

With a key, pass it as a bearer token:

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" \
  "https://www.sifintel.com/api/v1/nav?code=SIF-120&from=2025-10-01"

Terms in plain language

NAVs originate from AMFI and portfolio disclosures from the fund houses themselves; we normalise, verify and timestamp them. Licensing the feed gives you the right to use it internally and, on the Pro plan, to display it to your own users with attribution to SIFintel. We don't offer exclusivity, and we don't sell recommendations — this is data, not a view.
