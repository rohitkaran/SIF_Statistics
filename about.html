About SIFintel

SIFintel is an independent data and education platform for India's Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs). Our mission is simple: help investors and distributors understand this new asset class clearly and honestly.

SIFintel · About

Why we exist

SIFs are India's newest regulated investment category — powerful, but genuinely more complex than a mutual fund, and brand new. Reliable, neutral information is scarce. SIFintel was built to close that gap: to gather the data in one place and to explain how SIFs work in plain language, so that decisions are informed rather than sold.

What we do

  • Data. We track live AMFI NAVs for every Indian SIF and turn them into comparable returns, risk metrics and disclosed-portfolio views on our comparison dashboard.
  • Education for investors. Plain-language guides on what SIFs are, the product categories, and the SEBI framework — see Learn.
  • Education for distributors. Material to help mutual-fund distributors understand SIFs and prepare for the relevant certification, including free study primers with practice questions.
  • Context. A rolling feed of world financial news and our own market commentary, so the numbers can be read against what is happening in the world.
  • Videos. Short explainers we publish on YouTube and Instagram.

How we stay neutral

We do not recommend, rank, rate or promote any fund or AMC, and we do not accept payment to feature funds. Where we name funds or fund houses, it is only as neutral, factual examples. Our aim is understanding, not persuasion.

SIFintel is a service of Lume Software Private Limited. We are not a SEBI-registered investment adviser, research analyst or distributor. Everything we publish is for education only.
